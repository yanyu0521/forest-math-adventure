# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Phaser.js (requested); bundler and target browser support are open decisions.

## Users

Elementary school students practicing math.

## Product Purpose

An interactive math adventure lets students move a character to collect answers. Success means completing five levels while practicing arithmetic.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Five levels progress through addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and a mixed review.
- Collecting a correct answer adds 10 points; collecting a wrong answer subtracts 5 points.
- Each level contains three questions as an implementation choice; the intended grade range and number ranges remain open.

## Brand Commitments

- The user chose a bright forest adventure setting.

## Product Principles

- Let students practice by moving and making answer choices.
- Give immediate, legible feedback for each answer.
- Make the five-level progression easy to follow.
